    How much of the Attestcoin oracle is actually being used

    Before building on a protocol it is worth knowing who else is. So this measures it rather than guessing: every event the Attestcoin verifier precompile (0x…0FD2) emitted on CC3 testnet across a -day window, and which contract caused each one.

    What this measurement does and does not say

    It says what was measured: every verifier event in the window, with complete coverage and no gaps. It does not say who operates any particular address, and it does not claim the two highest-volume contracts are anyone in particular. What is visible is their shape: one sender each, one function selector each, in tight bursts. Draw your own conclusion.

    Reproduce it yourself with script/measure.ts in the repository. It is about forty lines and it talks to the public RPC.
